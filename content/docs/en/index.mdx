# Documentation

**GDD Manager** turns your messy notebook of ideas into a structured document that's navigable and pleasant to read. This documentation covers every part of the app, **why it exists**, and **how to get the most out of it** in your project.

## What's in the app

The document is a **tree of pages**. Each page stands for a subject in your game — a class, a mechanic, the economy, the roadmap — and carries a title, a block-based description and domain tags.

On top of that, the app gives you three ways to look at the same document:

1. **Edit mode** — the tree on the side and the block editor in the middle, for writing.
2. **Document mode** — the whole GDD as one continuous read, ready to send to someone.
3. **Mind map** — the same tree as a graph, so you can see structure and connections at a glance.

The app's real strength is **cross-references**: write `$[Page Name]` in your text and it becomes a link. That's what turns the document into a navigable web instead of a pile of files.
